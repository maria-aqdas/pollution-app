## 🌟 Overview

**CityAir** is an AI-powered environmental monitoring platform designed to provide transparent, multi-dimensional pollution intelligence for any city worldwide. While conventional tools focus strictly on ambient air quality index (AQI), CityAir holistically breaks down **7 critical dimensions of pollution** alongside actionable health advice, side-by-side city comparisons, and 5-year outlook projections! 🏙️✨

## 🌈 Key Features ✨

* 📡 **Live Environmental Data:** Fetches real-time air quality metrics ($PM_{2.5}$, $PM_{10}$, ozone) and weather parameters directly via Open-Meteo APIs.
* 🤖 **AI-Assisted Environmental Synthesis:** Leverages Gemini to synthesize complex environmental indicators into plain-language health recommendations and insights.
* 🌐 **Bilingual Accessibility:** Full native support for both **English** and **Urdu (اردو)** to maximize community awareness and accessibility.
* ⚖️ **Side-by-Side City Comparison:** Compare environmental metrics across any two global cities concurrently.
* 🔮 **5-Year Trend Outlook:** AI-driven projections highlighting future environmental risk trajectories based on baseline data and urbanization patterns.
* 📍 **Geolocation Detection:** Instant one-click local pollution lookup via browser geolocation.

## 🧪 Comprehensive Pollution Coverage (7 Vectors) 🔬

| Vector | Indicator | Impact & Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 💨 **Air** | Live $PM_{2.5}$, $PM_{10}$ | Deep respiratory risks, cardiovascular stress, and asthma triggers. |
| 💧 **Water** | AI Water Quality Index | Risks of waterborne illnesses, runoff contamination, and crop safety. |
| 🔥 **Thermal** | Heat Island & Surface Temp | Urban heat stress, high cooling demands, and ecological disruption. |
| 🌱 **Land** | Soil & Waste Degradation | Industrial byproduct risks, plastic accumulation, and soil degradation. |
| 🔊 **Noise** | Ambient Urban Decibels | Chronic stress, sleep disturbance, and cognitive impairment in children. |
| 💡 **Light** | Skyglow & Luminance | Circadian disruption, night sky obscuration, and wildlife disorientation. |
| ☢️ **Radioactive**| Baseline Background Exposure | Nuclear/industrial safety monitoring and ambient radiological health. |
